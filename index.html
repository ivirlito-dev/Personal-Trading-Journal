📖 Biblical Trading Affirmations

The goal is not just to become a profitable trader but to become a disciplined steward of what God has entrusted to you.

1. PATIENCE – Wait for God's Timing

• "I don't chase the market; I wait for my opportunity."

Ecclesiastes 3:1 – To everything there is a season.

• "Not every movement deserves my participation."

Proverbs 19:2 – Haste leads to mistakes.

• "I would rather miss a trade than force a bad one."

Psalm 37:7 – Be still before the Lord and wait patiently.

2. DISCIPLINE – Follow the Plan

• "I trade my plan, not my feelings."

1 Corinthians 14:40 – Let everything be done decently and in order.

• "No confirmation, no execution."

Proverbs 21:5 – The plans of the diligent lead to profit.

• "I don't need to trade every day; I need to trade correctly."

Proverbs 16:3 – Commit your work to the Lord.

3. RISK MANAGEMENT – Protect Your Capital

• "Protecting my capital is more important than chasing profit."

Luke 16:10 – Whoever is faithful in little is faithful in much.

• "I accept my risk before I enter the market."

Luke 14:28 – Count the cost before building.

• "I will never risk tomorrow's opportunity for today's greed."

Proverbs 22:3 – The prudent see danger and take refuge.

4. TRADING PSYCHOLOGY – Control Your Emotions

• "I will not trade out of fear, greed, or revenge."

2 Timothy 1:7 – God gave us a spirit of power, love, and self-control.

• "A missed trade is not a missed opportunity forever."

Philippians 4:11 – Learn to be content in every situation.

• "My peace is more valuable than any profit."

Philippians 4:6-7 – Let the peace of God guard your heart and mind.
